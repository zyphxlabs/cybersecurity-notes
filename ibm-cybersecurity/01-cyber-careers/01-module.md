## why cybersecurity matters

Cybersecurity is basically like a shield that keeps our information and systems safe from people who want to mess with it. Every day security people are dealing with new threats popping up and they have to figure out solutions on the spot which sounds stressful but also kinda interesting.

There are different paths you can take as a cybersecurity professional like being a security analyst who spots vulnerabilities or a cryptographer who deals with encrypting sensitive stuff or an ethical hacker who literally acts like an attacker to find weak points before real attackers do.

Almost every industry needs these people now not just tech companies. Even finance needs multiple types of security roles like someone to protect financial data during transactions someone to look into cybercrimes specifically in finance and someone to help set up secure systems and networks for the finance team.

Money wise this field pays pretty well because the responsibility is huge you're literally protecting an organizations digital stuff and staying updated with new tech is part of the job. According to US Bureau of Labor Statistics cybersecurity analyst jobs are expected to grow 35% by 2031 which is way faster than average jobs growth.

The reason demand keeps rising is because attacks keep getting worse and more creative ransomware locks your whole system until you pay up and phishing tricks people into giving away sensitive info willingly. As more businesses go digital people are also getting more careful about their data being safe so companies have to invest in security just to keep customer trust.

## entry level roles

- Cybersecurity Specialist sets up and manages security protocols does regular audits and jumps in when a breach happens
- Cybercrime Analyst looks at patterns in past attacks to predict future ones and does digital forensics after a breach happens reporting findings to stakeholders or law enforcement if needed
- Intrusion and Incident Analyst is the one who reacts when an intrusion is detected finds where it came from and how bad the damage is then works on containing and recovering from it
- IT Auditor checks if the organization's IT systems follow laws and industry standards and points out risks that need fixing

salary wise entry roles range roughly from 25k to 152k depending on which one median usually sits around 90 to 107k

## mid level roles

Once you gain more experience you move into mid level roles which need a much deeper understanding of how everything works together. These roles carry more responsibility especially around building and maintaining secure networks handling incidents and shaping policies.

- Cybersecurity Analyst manages the overall security structure enforces policies and also does vulnerability assessments and penetration testing along with helping in incident response planning
- Cybersecurity Consultant is more of an outside expert who audits an organizations systems finds weak spots and suggests fixes to senior management they also train employees on good security habits
- Penetration and Vulnerability Tester basically hacks the system on purpose but ethically to find weaknesses before real attackers do they document everything they find and suggest fixes afterward

pay range for mid level is around 45k to 188k depending on role which is honestly a decent jump from entry level

## advanced level roles

- Cybersecurity Manager oversees the whole cybersecurity program builds the team and handles incident response strategy at a higher level
- CISO is basically the top executive for security reports to CIO or CEO and is responsible for the entire security vision and culture of the company
- Cybersecurity Engineer is more hands on building and coding secure systems networks and architectures
- Cybersecurity Architect designs the big picture security framework deciding how all the different security pieces fit and work together

these advanced roles are basically the peak of the field and come with way more strategic decision making and leadership responsibility compared to entry or mid level
