## Security Frameworks & Controls

- Security frameworks are like guidelines that help us build plans to reduce risk and protect our data
- These frameworks are always changing because they follow a security lifecycle with policies and standards that evolve all the time
- There are four parts to these frameworks. We identify what we want to achieve we set some guidelines we put some processes in place and then we monitor what happens
- Controls are like safeguards that help reduce specific risks for example we might have mandatory privacy training to make sure everyone knows what to do
- Controls and frameworks work together they do not work separately from each other

## CIA Triad

- The CIA Triad is a model that people use to think about risk
- There are three main parts to the CIA Triad. Confidentiality, integrity and availability
- Confidentiality means that only authorized people can access our data
- Integrity means that our data is correct and has not been tampered with and encryption can help with this
- Availability means that authorized users can get to our data when they need it
- An asset is anything that is valuable to an organization and the more sensitive it is, the more protection it needs

## NIST CSF

- The NIST Cybersecurity Framework is a voluntary framework from the US government that helps manage cybersecurity risk
- Most security teams use the NIST Cybersecurity Framework as a baseline
- It is a good idea to know about the NIST Cybersecurity Framework because it comes up in interviews

## Common Compliance Standards

- The General Data Protection Regulation or GDPR is a law in the European Union that protects data and organizations have to notify users within 72 hours if there is a breach
- The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act or HIPAA is a law in the US that protects patient health information and organizations cannot share this information without consent
- The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard or PCI DSS is a standard for organizations that handle credit cards and it helps reduce fraud
- The Service Organization Control or SOC reports are used to check if organizations are complying with financial regulations and if user access is secure

## Ethics in Security

- Just because we have access to something does not mean that we should use it
- We have to stay unbiased even if the person involved is someone we know
- There are three things that we always have to respect. Confidentiality, privacy and the law
- In the US it is illegal to counterattack threat actors we can only defend ourselves
- We have to document everything follow the policy and not take any shortcuts when it comes to Security Frameworks and Controls and the CIA Triad and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and Common Compliance Standards and Ethics, in Security.
